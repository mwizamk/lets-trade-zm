FAQs

How do I order a subscription?

Select the service from the price list, continue to signup/order, and submit your customer details.

When do I receive account details?

Account access is provided after the order and payment have been verified by the administrator.

Can I have more than one service?

Yes. The signup page is designed to allow multiple services in one order.

What if my subscription expires?

Your dashboard should show the service as expired and active access should no longer be displayed.
